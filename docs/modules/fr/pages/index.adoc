= Crystal Kramdown AsciiDoc

Convertit du Markdown (sous-ensemble CommonMark) en format AsciiDoc. Port Crystal inspire de la gem Ruby https://github.com/asciidoctor/kramdown-asciidoc[kramdown-asciidoc] (v2.1.1).

== Conversions Prises en Charge

[cols="1,1"]
|===
| Markdown | AsciiDoc

| `# Titre` | `= Titre`
| `**gras**` | `*gras*`
| `*italique*` | `_italique_`
| Blocs de code | `[source]\n----`
| `[lien](url)` | `link:url[lien]`
| `![alt](url)` | `image:url[alt]`
| `> citation` | `____`
| `- element` | `* element`
| `1. ordonne` | `. ordonne`
| `---` ligne horizontale | `'''`
| Tableaux | Tableaux AsciiDoc
| `<!-- commentaire -->` | `////`
|===
